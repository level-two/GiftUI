# SPEC-009 T7.3 Handoff Corpus Evidence

Twelve canonical handoff cases cover accepted completion, all four legal
called-body failure pairings, all four no-body endpoint outcomes, and collapse
of every other pairing to contract violation. They retain exact producer errors
and distinguish endpoint refusal from render-producer refusal. Reservation is
observable before consumption and released on every non-acceptance; candidate
data is committed only on acceptance, otherwise discarded, and the borrowed
sink is poisoned after return. Operation-count mismatch fails closed, while
irreversible output is admitted only with accepted endpoint health.
