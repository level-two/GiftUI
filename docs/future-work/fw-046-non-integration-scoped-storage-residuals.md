# FW-046: Non-integration Scoped Storage Residuals

## Observation / Opportunity

Iteration-4 revision 12 narrows RFC-015 delivery to dependencies of the supported
external counter, recording adapter and reusable-host Signal Analyzer. Full
RFC-wide completion can include additional caller, documentation or evidence
residuals outside those compositions. No concrete residual is declared safely
excluded by this capture alone.

## Why Deferred

The maintainer requested focused integration delivery on 2026-11-08. I4-07 must
classify every B1–B4 / adopted ownership-criterion residual in W0 and reconcile it
in W4. An exclusion requires an exact caller/criterion/source/evidence row and a
reason it is not needed by any adopted shared or application path. Required
safety, compatibility, lifetime, cleanup and resource proof remain in iteration 4.

## Potential Value

Finish full-contract conformance for additional consumers without placing
unrelated delivery on the integration critical path.

## Current Non-goals

No weaker RFC/ADR/Spec contract, automatic iteration-5 assignment, inferred full
conformance, experiment allowance or implementation authorization. The actual
analyzer model/root/target/factory and owned-return residual remains I4-07 work.

## Revisit Triggers

- I4-07 W0/W4 classification identifies a concrete non-integration residual:
  append its exact identity, missing evidence and owner here or link its ledger.
- A new consumer depends on an excluded path, or full SPEC-011/013/015 conformance
  is requested: re-evaluate all affected residuals through normal owner plans.
- Evidence shows a supposed exclusion affects an iteration-4 adopted path:
  return it immediately to I4-07 as a required dependency.

## Disposition

Captured under Eugene's 2026-11-08 instruction to narrow iteration 4 as
recommended. Detailed exclusions are pending dependency classification, not
assumed. Full Specification criteria remain unmet wherever required coverage is
missing; partial integration acceptance cannot mark them implemented.
